Does Topical Magnesium Actually Do Anything, or Is It Just Hype?

Topical magnesium absorption is real, though it works differently than swallowing a pill. When you apply magnesium oil to skin, magnesium ions can pass through hair follicles and sweat glands into underlying tissue. This matters because many people who take oral magnesium supplements experience diminishing returns from digestive absorption limits or mild gastrointestinal discomfort at higher doses.

Magnesium plays a role in activating GABA receptors in the brain, the same receptors that help quiet neural activity and ease the transition into deep sleep. It also interacts with the regulation of cortisol, the stress hormone that tends to spike when your body is under-recovered or depleted. Think of cortisol like a volume knob turned too high at night. When magnesium levels are adequate, that knob has more room to come down before your head hits the pillow.
